Since 2021

Crafting Perfect Moments

We started as a small coffee cart on the corner of the street. Today, deGudang is a sanctuary for coffee lovers, thinkers, and creators looking for premium quality brews and a serene space to unwind.

Every single cup is meticulously measured, ground, and extracted by baristas who genuinely care about the craft of coffee making.
